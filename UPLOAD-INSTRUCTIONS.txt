CARMIND FINAL TABLET UPLOAD — 2 ANDROID APPS

Upload these TWO files to the main/root page of the GitHub repository:

1. Carmind-project.zip
2. codemagic.yaml

Do not open Carmind-project.zip and do not upload its internal folders.
Both files must appear on the first page of the GitHub repository, beside each other.

CodeMagic reads codemagic.yaml, opens Carmind-project.zip during the build, and produces:

- app-debug.apk = Carmind Car for the Android car screen
- phone-debug.apk = Carmind Mobile for the normal Android phone

This build downloads the free Apache-2.0 Vosk English voice model during
CodeMagic compilation. The finished APKs are therefore larger than before.

This revision also routes Carmind replies as Android navigation/assistant
speech. Android Auto or Bluetooth media should duck while Carmind speaks and
return to its previous level when the reply ends.

Customers can choose Carmind Priority or Android Auto Compatible under
Settings > Car audio control. Carmind Priority pauses competing audio while
Carmind speaks and includes a shortcut to Android Auto's system settings.

The default is Android Auto Compatible so music continues at a lower volume
while Carmind speaks. The package also contains the three-hour server-backed
trial, monthly/yearly subscription verification, owner Exit PIN, Nigerian
English command normalisation, contact calling, connected-phone music,
incoming-call status, and voice control of the Carmind media library.

IMPORTANT TEST LIMITS

- Install app-debug.apk on the Android car screen.
- Install phone-debug.apk on the normal Android phone.
- Android may warn about a sideloaded debug app because calling, microphone,
  contacts and Bluetooth are sensitive permissions. A public customer release
  must be signed and distributed through a trusted store/testing track.
- WhatsApp notification reading is intentionally excluded from the debug APK
  because sideloaded notification-listener apps are commonly blocked. Keep the
  source integration for the properly signed production release.
- YouTube and Spotify decide whether an external track may autoplay. Carmind
  can open the requested result, but cannot bypass those apps' account or
  playback policies.
