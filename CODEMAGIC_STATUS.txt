Important Android limitation:
Android 14+ prohibits a BOOT_COMPLETED receiver from directly starting a microphone foreground service. CarMind therefore posts a one-tap "CarMind AI is ready" notification after boot. Once the user opens/activates CarMind, the foreground listening service can remain active subject to the car unit's battery-management rules.

True low-power 24/7 hotword detection should eventually use a dedicated on-device wake-word engine. Android SpeechRecognizer is retained here as the first functional voice path, but Android itself documents that SpeechRecognizer is not intended for indefinite continuous recognition.
