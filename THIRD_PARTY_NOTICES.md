## Vosk Android

Carmind AI uses `com.alphacephei:vosk-android:0.3.75` and the Vosk
`vosk-model-en-us-0.22-lgraph` English model as its on-device speech
recognizer.

The Vosk component is used only for recognising microphone input. Carmind's
selected Android text-to-speech voice remains responsible for spoken replies.
